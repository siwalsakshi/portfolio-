Ago-Carpooling: Ride Sharing Android Application

Flutter Dart Firebase Firestore Cloud Functions UPI Payments Google Play Console

An end-to-end carpooling solution that connects commuters travelling along similar routes, cutting transport costs, reducing traffic emissions, and delivering live tracking and seamless UPI payments.

View on Google Play Store Explore GitHub

🎯 Executive Summary

Urban transit in growing metropolitan areas faces severe congestion, inflated cab fares, and limited shared-mobility alternatives. Ago-Carpooling was conceived and developed to provide a reliable, cost-efficient, and peer-to-peer ride sharing platform specifically optimized for daily corporate and university commuters.

I spearheaded the mobile frontend development in Flutter (Dart), engineered backend cloud services via Firebase, integrated real-time location and chat services, and successfully guided the application through Google Play Store policy review to production launch on October 2, 2026.

✨ Architectural Highlights & Features

Multi-Factor Authentication

Integrated Firebase Auth with Google OAuth, SMS OTP verification, and email authentication for secure user onboarding and driver verification.

Dynamic Ride Posting

Drivers can publish upcoming trips specifying origin, destination, departure time, seat capacity, vehicle model, and cost per seat.

Live Route Tracking

Integrated Google Maps SDK with background GPS tracking to show real-time driver coordinates and dynamic ETA recalculations.

In-App UPI Payments

Direct intent-based UPI gateway integration enabling seamless and instant bank-to-bank passenger payments with transaction reconciliation.

Real-Time Chat

Instant messaging between driver and booked passengers built on Firestore real-time snapshot listeners with push notifications.

Play Store Deployment

Complete release engineering including App Bundle generation, Keystore signing, privacy compliance, and store listing optimization.

⚡ Key Engineering Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Race Conditions in Seat Reservations

When multiple commuters simultaneously attempt to reserve the last available seat on a popular route, naive updates resulted in overbooking anomalies.

→ Solution: Implemented Firestore Atomic Transactions (`runTransaction`) with conditional checks to lock the document, ensure available seats are strictly greater than zero, and atomically decrement seat counts while creating the booking record.

Challenge: Battery Drain During Continuous Location Tracking

Continuous high-frequency GPS polling significantly impacted device battery life during long commutes.

→ Solution: Designed an adaptive location throttling algorithm that scales location updates based on vehicle speed and proximity to upcoming pickup coordinates (5-second intervals when near stops, 20-second intervals on highways).
